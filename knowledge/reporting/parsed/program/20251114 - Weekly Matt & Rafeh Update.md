**Proud:**

**Coding Nova:** The DS/MLOps team (4 members from MIAP) participated in the G&D Coding Nova Hackathon, developing a booking solution that integrates ChatGPT and Claude Desktop with an MCP server equipped with cruise booking tools. This prototype enables users to book cruises directly through the LLM interface while providing real-time, accurate itinerary, pricing, and ship details—solving the common issue of outdated third-party data and demonstrating the potential for AI-driven customer experiences.

Additionally, a 26-month sailing propensity model was developed and deployed to enable identification of high-value consumers likely to sail within a given timeframe, with scores and dashboards accessible to stakeholders to optimize marketing campaigns. *Teams are now focusing on refining active consumer targeting models for sustained effectiveness.*

**•Loyalty Program Redesign:** Teams completed a Credit Card Acquisition Impact analysis, providing insights into loyalty-driven spending behaviors and tier migration potential, integral to evaluating multi-brand engagement and spend-based incentives.
