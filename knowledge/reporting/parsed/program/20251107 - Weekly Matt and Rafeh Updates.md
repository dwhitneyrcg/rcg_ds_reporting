**Concerns:**

**•****Revenue Management: **PRE recommendations and several TAP projects were halted this week due to a critical Oracle-Databricks connector upgrade that truncated all floating-point numbers to whole numbers, corrupting track and currency exchange data in Oracle pulls. This marks the first time this year that PRE pricing recommendations were not pushed to AS400. The issue was only detected during an AUD PRE review over the weekend by a Revenue Product Analyst, highlighting a serious gap in proactive QA. Although Data Engineering corrected the Alpha data within days and revenue impact was fortunately limited here, the lack of early detection exposes us to the risk of catastrophic errors going unnoticed, potentially causing significant revenue loss. **Strengthening data quality monitoring and implementing rigorous QA processes is urgent to prevent future incidents.**
