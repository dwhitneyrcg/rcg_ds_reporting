CEL Revenue Management | Price Change Logic
Implemented variable-norm logic in the price_change notebook for CEL.
Compared price recommendations using constant norm, variable norm, and no normalization for CET and RCI.
RCI showed many extreme price-change cases (±100%): 43% of cases were extreme with no normalization, 14% with constant norm, and 12% with variable norm. Variable norm produced a well-normalized distribution and yielded acceptable, aligned recommendations for RCI.
For CEL, 18% of cases were extreme without normalization. Applying either constant or variable normalization removed almost all extreme cases and produced very similar distributions.
