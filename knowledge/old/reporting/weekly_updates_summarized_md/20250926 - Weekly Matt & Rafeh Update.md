### Marine Insights Analytics Platform (Marine Operations)

**Date:** 2025-09-26
**Business Area:** Marine Insights Analytics Platform (Marine Operations)
**Business Project:** Fleetwide Energy Monitoring

**Achievements:**
DS/MLOps Teams held important meetings with the American Bureau of Shipping to define measurable safety KPIs for MIAP and initiated discussions with the Supply Chain IBP team to explore approaches for marine supply chain optimization.
