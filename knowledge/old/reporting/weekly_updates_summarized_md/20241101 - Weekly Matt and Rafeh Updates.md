### Loyalty

**Date:** 2024-11-01
**Business Area:** Loyalty
**Business Project:** Loyalty Simulator Framework

**Achievements:**
The team completed validation of the backward simulator model and did not discover any issues. Validation and additional analyses were necessary to determine the root cause for lower tier sizes for sailed RCI guests under the new program design. Instead, the team discovered that a higher proportion of loyalty members under the new program rules will not sail RCI (either sailing CEL/SSC instead or being inactive due to our generous requalification rules). Based on these findings, the team may need to re-optimize tier thresholds to maintain tier sizes for sailed guests, rather than based on total loyalty members.
