### PCP Pricing Automation (RCI/CEL)

**Date:** 2026-02-27
**Business Area:** PCP Pricing Automation (RCI/CEL)
**Business Project:** Perfect Day Product Pricing

**Achievements:**
Teams aligned the business on adopting a controlled A/B testing framework for Waterpark pricing, rather than deploying a rules-based PRE, given the high risk of price-driven demand interventions when demand is likely inelastic and could negatively impact yields. With limited booking data showing apparent inelasticity but early signals of potential elasticity at lower price points, teams agreed that testing is the only defensible path to determine whether lowering prices truly increases total yield versus locking in higher prices that suppress volume. Also, the team modernized Alaska Shore Excursions reporting by rebuilding the dashboard on a new booking source with dynamic year-over-year comparisons, completed Waterpark pricing and elasticity analysis by ship class and seasonality, and laid the groundwork for CRF automation through dynamic product base tables and an RBC occupancy (% to capacity) metric to enable scalable, rules-driven pricing.

**Focus Areas:**
Teams are focusing on completing end-to-end offer visibility testing in QA/dev, validating CRF business rules with OBR, and hardening deployment pipelines (including CI/CD and repo separation) to support repeatable releases.
