### Marine Insights Analytics Platform (Marine Operations)

**Date:** 2025-11-14
**Business Area:** Marine Insights Analytics Platform (Marine Operations)
**Business Project:** Advanced Modeling Development

**Achievements:**
DS/MLOPs Teams finalized the integration of ML and EQ capabilities into the MIAP IoT platform and optimized the Digital Twin API deployment architecture, enabling efficient voyage simulation and stability modeling for analytics. Significant advancements include the completion of propulsion tuning and hull degradation models for fleet-wide fuel forecasting, compliance with AI ethics concerns, and the refinement of ALS compressor power prediction models, all aligned to reducing energy costs and CO2e emissions.

**Focus Areas:**
Teams are now focusing on refining stability agents, service power models, chiller analytics, and addressing deployment challenges across workflows and HVAC systems, ensuring readiness for future deliverables.
