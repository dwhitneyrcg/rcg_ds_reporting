### Revenue Management Automation (RCI)

**Date:** 2025-12-08
**Business Area:** Revenue Management Automation (RCI)
**Business Project:** GTY-Lead Fare Optimization Model

**Achievements:**
Teams presented at RCI’s RM All-Hands to introduce value-adding Generative AI use-cases with MSFT Co-pilot to the broader department. Teams updated the reporting pipelines for PRE with enhanced pricing recommendation metrics and weekly optimization comparisons to refine decision-making in alignment with revenue targets. Excited:
•Contact Center Optimization & Automation (RCI/CEL): Teams delivered testing and validation updates for BK2CX pipelines, including lead scoring backup path configurations, implementing agency suppression logic, and updates for handling delayed agency leads, ensuring enhanced outbound conversion metrics. Efforts addressed several bug fixes and data accuracy issues, including service power model improvements across multiple ships, contributing to better energy performance monitoring.

**Focus Areas:**
Teams are focusing on Workforce Planning app migration to Azure Container Apps and integrating call volume forecasts into International and Casino workforce tools. Teams are now conducting segmentation of SHAP values and manual analysis to isolate causal factors for embedding actionable insights into campaigns. Teams are now focused on resolving deployment issues, finalizing model optimizations, and enhancing API connectivity across MIAP systems to support broader fleetwide monitoring objectives.
