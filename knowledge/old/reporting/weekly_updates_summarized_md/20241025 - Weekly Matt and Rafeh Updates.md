### Marine Operations

**Date:** 2024-10-25
**Business Area:** Marine Operations
**Business Project:** Historical Data Integration

**Achievements:**
DS Team continuing to work on new digital twin model framework and implementing engine-fuel models for fuel forecasting. Also, team identified MIAP as cost savings alternative to "Sail Plan" companies IoT platform. Sail Plan provides CO2e analytics which reduces calculated CO2 emissions by installing sensos on engine exhaust pipes and direct measurement of CO2 instead of converting fuel consumption to CO2 measurements. Free pilot project is starting on one ship. Cost savings (TBD) compared to using Sail Plan's IoT platform to collect data. NPS Target Setting: DS Team developing a Multi-Modelling NPS Forecasting model and Model Explainability metrics to help business understand the NPS forecasts. Business expressed confidence in our forecasts and pending last round of validation, is planning to use DS targets for 2025.
