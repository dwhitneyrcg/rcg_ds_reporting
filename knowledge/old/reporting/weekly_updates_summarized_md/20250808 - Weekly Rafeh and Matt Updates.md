### Hybris Product Recommendations (Digital)

**Date:** 2025-08-08
**Business Area:** Hybris Product Recommendations (Digital)
**Business Project:** MyCruise Product Recommender Testing

**Achievements:**
DS, MLOPs, and Digital Teams launched MyCruise product recommendations to 5% of Digital traffic with add-to-cart and PLP testing over the weekend. These recommendations leverage purchase and clickstream history across three model types—trending, association rules, and collaborative filtering—supported by an endpoint capable of serving 3,000 requests per second. Team also delivered unit testing for all recommendation models, blocking new model deployments if they fail.

**Focus Areas:**
Teams are now working on retraining segmentation models and refining APIs to enhance recommendation accuracy and scalability.

### Marine Insights Analytics Platform (Marine Operations)

**Date:** 2025-08-08
**Business Area:** Marine Insights Analytics Platform (Marine Operations)
**Business Project:** HVAC Diagnostics & Anomaly Detection

**Achievements:**
DS and MLOPs teams updated MIAP fuel savings to $5.65M (or $1.55M for 2025), after bug fixes and closing tickets on SmartSheet issue tracker. DS Team heavily emphasized identifying energy savings this week identifying $81K in potential savings across five ships using AHU anomaly models (193kW), and identified additional efficiency opportunities on 4 ships through other HVAC diagnostics (of >500 kW). Key advancements this week include the rollout of advanced regression models & enhancing of the Stability Chatbot (using Claude 4.0) for the Symphony Stability Data Analysis project and refactoring of the Fuel Forecast API for improved code reusability. Teams are progressing on MIAP ETL optimizations, vessel data diagnostics, and integrating advanced modeling techniques for energy and anomaly predictions.
