### Revenue Management Automation (RCI)

**Date:** 2025-11-21
**Business Area:** Revenue Management Automation (RCI)
**Business Project:** GTY-Lead Fare Optimization Model

**Achievements:**
DS Team presented the Category Gapping 4.0 strategy with positive feedback from RM Leadership. We will be focusing on optimizing physical categories and avoiding price differences between lower categories. DS/MLOPs delivered updated automation processes in Databricks, enhancing operational efficiency and reducing costs, and finalized pricing analysis for last-minute promotions to improve revenue predictability. Inventory Automation processes have been fully migrated out of ADF, with T4 Limits now in Databricks and ADA Berthing upgraded for Cat Class, streamlining operations and opening up the ADF production push schedule. Ongoing work includes auditing FIT track inputs, advancing promotion impact analysis for booking windows, and preparing to deploy the updated price optimization method after the holiday, while also addressing prediction errors and enhancing data validation frameworks. For SPI, the team is aligning scoring models with yields for five ships, experimenting with features like seasonal capacity, school calendars, holiday inputs, and macroeconomic trends to improve model accuracy without unnecessary complexity.
