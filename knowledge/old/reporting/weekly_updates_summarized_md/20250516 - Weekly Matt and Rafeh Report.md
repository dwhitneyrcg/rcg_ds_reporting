### RoyalOne Community (Digital)

**Date:** 2025-05-16
**Business Area:** RoyalOne Community (Digital)
**Business Project:** App Engagement Revenue Analysis

**Achievements:**
Teams completed a correlational analysis on app engagement and shared the results with Digital Leadership this week (receiving positive feedback). The analysis, using advanced regression modeling (like we did in RM), demonstrates that higher levels of app engagement (a) predicts higher onboard spend (PCP + OBR) and (b) is linked to new-to-cruise guests (without any prior cruise spend) spending more like experienced cruisers. While exciting, these results are correlational and not causal. We cannot conclude a causal linkage between guest spend and app engagement.

**Focus Areas:**
Next steps are to simplify the messaging for executive audiences.
