### Marine Insights Analytics Platform (Marine Operations)

**Date:** 2025-07-25
**Business Area:** Marine Insights Analytics Platform (Marine Operations)
**Business Project:** Fleetwide Energy Monitoring

**Achievements:**
Teams delivered an AHU cooling power workflow update and completed deployment of the MIAP IoT flow for Explorer of the Seas, enhancing energy monitoring capabilities and fleetwide integration. Additionally, a casino table games anomaly detection model was prepared for deployment as the first Edge streaming ML model onboard. Also identified potential energy savings for up to 250kW in HVAC AHU anomalies across 3 ships (CS, SY, and ML).

**Focus Areas:**
Teams are focusing on refining fuel forecasting models, improving MIAP ETL workflows, and analyzing high power consumption cases in chillers and AHUs to identify cost-saving opportunities.
