### Revenue Management Automation (CEL)

**Date:** 2025-06-20
**Business Area:** Revenue Management Automation (CEL)
**Business Project:** GTY-Lead Fare Optimization Model 3.0

**Achievements:**
DS Team delivered an initial draft of the joint 2025 RCI/CEL RMA CAR with constructive feedback from the RCI & CEL Leadership teams, namely on the area to emphasize RM analyst user experience with automation and bi-branded RMA governance model (i.e. ways of working). Team also delivered SPI scoring model improvements, including a shift from multiplicative to additive normalization to enhance accuracy, and productionized Pre-Elasticity upgrades by successfully merging retraining workflows into QA pipelines.

**Focus Areas:**
Teams are now focused on scaling inter-category gap optimizations across all sailings and refining seasonal demand error metrics for more accurate booking forecasts.
