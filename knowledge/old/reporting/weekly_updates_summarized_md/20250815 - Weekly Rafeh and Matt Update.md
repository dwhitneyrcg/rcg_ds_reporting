### Contact Center Optimization & Automation (RCI/CEL)

**Date:** 2025-08-15
**Business Area:** Contact Center Optimization & Automation (RCI/CEL)
**Business Project:** Cresta AI Analytics Tools

**Achievements:**
DS Team delivered optimized probability mapping for Royal CTI leads and automated daily email reporting on lead conversion, improving accuracy for outbound outreach teams.
