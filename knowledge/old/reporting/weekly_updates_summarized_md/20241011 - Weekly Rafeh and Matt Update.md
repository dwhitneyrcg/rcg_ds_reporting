### Loyalty

**Date:** 2024-10-11
**Business Area:** Loyalty
**Business Project:** Co-Brand Credit Card Pilot

**Focus Areas:**
Current Objective: include SSC in simulator
Rework Clusters:
Continuing EDA on new spend based clustering algorithm including SSC data. Guests are now assigned to clusters for each sailing based on their behavior over the prior two years at the time of each sailing. This is a large improvement from the point-in-time snap shot clusters previously used that assign guests to one cluster, since individual guest behavior changes over time.
The first pass at this is a tri-branded clustering approach, including a variety of spend driving features. Delineation between clusters is unclear, likely driven by large differences in guest spend and behavior across the brands.
Next step is to create separate clusters for the brands and compare results.
Create Spend Model:
Created a POC spend model that predicts guest spend based on prior guest behavior as well as itinerary driven features.
The prior approach sampled guest spend from the cluster distribution between the 15th and 85th percentiles. This relied too heavily on the clusters and did not account for any itinerary effects or past guest behavior.
Initial findings are very promising, predicting guest spend with 28% Mdape in backtested periods for RCI & CEL. The model also weights the contribution of Deloitte's clusters quite low, which validates the new approach further. We expect cluster contribution to increase after the new clusters are deployed.
