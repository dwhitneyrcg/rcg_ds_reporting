### Win-on-Waste

**Date:** 2024-05-10
**Business Area:** Win-on-Waste
**Business Project:** Specialty Dining Optimization

**Achievements:**
This week the WoW team favorably reported on a two-week backest test of AI Demand Forecast for select Specialty Dining Venues on one ship (Freedom of the Seas, 2/9/24 - 2/23/24). The team's AI demand forecasts closely aligned with the PoS actuals and, if followed, would have saved the company $11k. If these savings can be replicated across the entire fleet and maintained across the entire year, the projected annual savings from the demand forecast would be $6.9M annually for RCI.

**Focus Areas:**
Next steps are to productionize the model and make them available to shipboard chefs.

### Loyalty

The joint DS/E-Commerce/Deloitte team presented a major progress update on the Phase 1 MVP Loyalty Simulator to Nancy and Andrea with positive feedback.
