### Revenue Management Automation (CEL)

**Date:** 2025-08-29
**Business Area:** Revenue Management Automation (CEL)
**Business Project:** Category Gapping Optimization 3.0

**Achievements:**
DS Team DS Team successfully fixed Inventory Automation for RCI & CEL (FIT Berthing / Replenishment) that was accidentally raising GTY limits due to AS400 system shortcomings. The team closely collaborated with IT and RM for identifying a quick solution and cleverly rescheduled inventory automation processes to bypass the error.
