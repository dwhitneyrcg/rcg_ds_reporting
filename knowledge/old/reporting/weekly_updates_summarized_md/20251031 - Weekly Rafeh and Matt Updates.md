### Hybris Product Recommendations (Digital)

**Date:** 2025-10-31
**Business Area:** Hybris Product Recommendations (Digital)
**Business Project:** Enhanced For You Recommendations

**Achievements:**
E-Commerce team, in conjunction with the AI Team, successfully launched the Royal Caribbean Shore Excursions Recommendation Campaign, enabling personalized excursion suggestions through Salesforce-integrated email marketing with dynamic, real-time updates based on guest plans—targeting enhanced PCP revenue through personalized recommendations. Additionally, the Calendar Recommendation engine was deployed to the dev endpoint on Azure PostgreSQL to lay scalability foundation, though firewall issues are impeding functionality and require resolution. The team is continuing efforts to migrate all production processes to prod environments.

### Marine Insights Analytics Platform (Marine Operations)

**Date:** 2025-10-31
**Business Area:** Marine Insights Analytics Platform (Marine Operations)
**Business Project:** HVAC Diagnostics & Anomaly Detection

**Achievements:**
AI Team attended the annual Decarbonization workshop, discussing MIAP Phase 5 and 6 funding and achieving alignment to allocate funds for newbuilds starting after 2027 to bolster team capabilities.
