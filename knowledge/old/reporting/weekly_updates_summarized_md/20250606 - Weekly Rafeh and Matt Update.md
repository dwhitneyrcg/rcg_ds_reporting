### Marine Insights Analytics Platform (Marine Operations)

**Date:** 2025-06-06
**Business Area:** Marine Insights Analytics Platform (Marine Operations)
**Business Project:** HVAC Diagnostics & Anomaly Detection

**Achievements:**
Teams resolved a $18K pump problem on Wonder of the Seas (200kW ) and finalized the electrical power module of the HVAC diagnostics platform, training 3,108 models to identify energy inefficiencies in Air Handling Units (AHUs) across 17 ships, with measurable savings already demonstrated on two ships (Infinity and Summit). Additionally, a cost monitoring dashboard reduced MIAP cloud spend by ~3% annually and multi-threading enhancements has reduced response time of the MIAP API by 60%.

**Focus Areas:**
Teams are actively investigating two machinery-related deviations for potential energy savings (300kW anomaly on AD and 180kW anomaly on ID) and testing the power plant base model, addressing anomalies in fuel forecasting outputs, and enhancing MIAP ETL pipelines.
