### Customer Lifetime Value (Corporate Planning)

**Date:** 2025-10-03
**Business Area:** Customer Lifetime Value (Corporate Planning)
**Business Project:** Cross-Brand Credit Card Strategy

**Achievements:**
DS team developed a long-term CLV integration strategy and presented a cobranded credit card analysis, revealing that avid cruisers, while only 3% of RCI’s population, represent 33% of cardholders. Insights via cruise experience and card status plots are informing strategies for identifying high-value segments.

**Focus Areas:**
Teams are now building a Power BI dashboard for granular demand analysis, restructuring cruise experience logic, integrating casino population validation, and expanding DMA analysis with additional air data.

### Hybris Product Recommendations (Digital)

**Date:** 2025-10-03
**Business Area:** Hybris Product Recommendations (Digital)
**Business Project:** MyCruise Product Recommender Testing

**Achievements:**
DS and Digital teams met with onboard revenue stakeholders and received strong support for the MyCruise recommender models. Early testing by the Digital Teams indicates the tested models alone will conservatively generate well over $8M in incremental gross PCP revenue through improved product conversion. This week MLOPs proposed a high-availability architecture for the MyCruise recommendations API. ### Contact Center Optimization & Automation (RCI/CEL)

**Date:** 2025-10-03
**Business Area:** Contact Center Optimization & Automation (RCI/CEL)
**Business Project:** Lead Prioritization - CTI (RCI & CEL)

**Achievements:**
DS & MLOPs Teams delivered several improvements this week for RMA. DS Team upgraded Elasticity model was tested, approved, and deployed into the Pricing Recommendation Engine (PRE), resolving key pricing anomalies, while MLOPs reconfigured Databricks connections for faster Power BI load times and shut down legacy ADF jobs to reduce 160hrs of compute usage. DS Team also made significant progress in refactoring the SPI scoring model to reduce overfitting. Teams are addressing GTY-Lead optimization debugging, continuing to refine the SPI scoring model, and optimize workflows and algorithms for PRE and on-demand berthing automation.

### Revenue Management Automation (SSC)

**Date:** 2025-10-03
**Business Area:** Revenue Management Automation (SSC)
**Business Project:** Pricing Recommendation Engine (PRE) Automation

**Achievements:**
DS Team enhanced the PRE pause file by adding past prices and implementation dates, saving SSC Revenue teams significant manual effort and improving decision-making; a new 3-week booking window business rule was also implemented, streamlining recommendations and reducing manual lookups. Additionally, the team resolved critical "Data Leakage" issues in the PRE validation file, ensuring complete data uploads to the reservation system and preventing price point rejections.

**Focus Areas:**
Teams are now focusing on resolving remaining validation errors to improve automation stability.
