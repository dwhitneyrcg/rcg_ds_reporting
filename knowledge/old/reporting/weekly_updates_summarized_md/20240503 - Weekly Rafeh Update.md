# Weekly Update (Summarized) - 2024-05-03

## Proud

### Win-on-Waste

**Date:** 2024-05-03
**Business Area:** Win-on-Waste
**Business Project:** Specialty Dining Optimization

**Achievements:**
This week the WoW team favorably reported on a two-week backest test of AI Demand Forecast for select Specialty Dining Venues on one ship (Freedom of the Seas, 2/9/24 - 2/23/24). The team's AI demand forecasts closely aligned with the PoS actuals and, if followed, would have saved the company $11k. If these savings can be replicated across the entire fleet and maintained across the entire year, the projected annuals savings from the demand forecast would be $6.9M annually for RCI.

**Focus Areas:**
Next steps are to productionize the model and make them available to shipboard chefs.

### Loyalty

**Date:** 2024-05-03
**Business Area:** Loyalty
**Business Project:** Loyalty Simulator Framework

**Achievements:**
The joint DS/E-Commerce/Deloitte team presented a major progress update on the Phase 1 MVP Loyalty Simulator to Nancy and Andrea with positive feedback. The team provided a perspective on (a) what thresholds (and corresponding member distribution) will effectively incentivize return, frequency, and overall member spend for a 5-tier program and (b) how adjusting the lower bound for Tier 1 (creating an “Enrolled Member” category) affects overall tier distributions. Next steps will be to assess profitability of the program based on projected Tier Benefits.

### Loyalty

**Date:** 2024-05-03
**Business Area:** Loyalty
**Business Project:** Loyalty Simulator Framework

**Achievements:**
The key topic will be presenting analysis for introducing a new “Enrollment Tier” or Tier 0, where consumers must spend a minimum amount to advance to Tier 1 and begin gaining Tier benefits (much like the airlines limit benefits to one-time consumers). The updated Tier Thresholds have been generated by the Loyalty Simulator, which is enabling RCG to rigorously test multiple program design scenarios to find the most optimal configuration by toggling program design parameters. Future work will focus on redemption and layering in benefits costs.

**Focus Areas:**
