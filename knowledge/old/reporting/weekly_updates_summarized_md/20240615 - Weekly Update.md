### Marine Operations

**Date:** 2024-06-15
**Business Area:** Marine Operations
**Business Project:** Fleetwide Energy Monitoring

**Achievements:**
Marine and Finances teams from RCI + CEL have agreed to support a joint partnership with Chantiers de Atlantique ship yard to share data from the MIAP web application (i.e. AI model outputs and raw IoT sensor data) as a tool for continuous monitoring of ship performance by yard energy experts instead of relying on yard's own solution.
