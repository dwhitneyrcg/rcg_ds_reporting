### Loyalty Program Redesign

**Date:** 2025-09-12
**Business Area:** Loyalty Program Redesign
**Business Project:** Choice Benefits Pilot

**Achievements:**
Team presented initial findings on the Choice Benefits Pilot to the SteerCo, showing early indications of increased bookings for treatment groups, though statistical significance is not yet determined. SteerCo approved testing both credit card (CC) spend and acquisition metrics for the next pilot, despite data limitations for statistically significant acquisition testing.
