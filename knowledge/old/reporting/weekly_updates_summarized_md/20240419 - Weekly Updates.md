### Contact Center Optimization & Automation (RCI/CEL)

**Date:** 2024-04-19
**Business Area:** Contact Center Optimization & Automation (RCI/CEL)

**Achievements:**
The team is partnering with Data Engineering & RCI/CEL Finance teams on a $200K proposal to automate shipboard gratuity workbooks. Today crew gratuities are manually recorded and tracked in complicated shipboard excel workbooks, which are difficult for our Finance teams to audit and maintain. We are proposing to create software automation that will (a) automatically pull relevant gratuity data from different source systems, (b) create reproducible excel workbooks, and (c) auto-populate the workbook with gratuities recorded in our POS system. The proposed work will greatly benefit gratuity workbook generation by improving accuracy and compliance, enabling auditability, and increasing labor efficiency.
