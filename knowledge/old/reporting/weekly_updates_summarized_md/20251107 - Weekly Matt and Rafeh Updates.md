### Supply Chain Optimization

**Date:** 2025-11-07
**Business Area:** Supply Chain Optimization
**Business Project:** CocoCay Integration and Guardrails

**Achievements:**
Teams delivered the lowest-ever model error for a single month in the next-generation HF&B demand forecast model development, reducing error by 15%. The forecasting error rate now stands at a very low 15% (from 18%), which the industry standard according to Jim Wells hovers around 50-60%. Uniforms pipeline updates included completion of multiple logic steps for SSC versions and continued optimization of medical inventory handling and finance tool automation, enhancing operational scalability. Excited:
•Customer Targeting (E-Commerce): DS Teams finished exploratory analysis for building a “Guest Journey Stage” model (i.e. “how close we think they are to making a booking”) and completed stakeholder requested updates to our “Sailing Horizon” model (i.e. “how far out guests are likely to book”). Code refactoring efforts have enhanced maintainability through shared configurations and improved documentation. Digital Bot Support: DS Team delivered an updated deployment notebook and finalized the production deployment of a topic classification pipeline for survey analytics, significantly reducing processing time and improving data granularity through parallelized API calls and new topic additions. Additionally, critical escalation-flagging logic for guest services chatbot interactions was captured, with integration work underway to enhance dashboard visibility of escalation metrics.

**Focus Areas:**
Teams are now focused on advancing backtests for HF&B models, refining data dependencies for inventory dashboards, completing CocoCay demand file merging, and addressing data refreshes/blockers in Uniform consumption calculations. Team is now researching multi-task model architectures to predict additional attributes, such as the most likely Product, Cabin Class, and Sailing Propensity concurrently for E-Commerce/Loyalty Simulator use cases. Teams are now developing the escalation logic into the production pipeline while updating supporting metrics dashboards.

## Concerned

### Revenue Management Automation (RCI)

**Date:** 2025-11-07
**Business Area:** Revenue Management Automation (RCI)
**Business Project:** PRE 4.0 Elasticity Enhancements

**Focus Areas:**
PRE recommendations and several TAP projects were halted this week due to a critical Oracle-Databricks connector upgrade that truncated all floating-point numbers to whole numbers, corrupting track and currency exchange data in Oracle pulls. This marks the first time this year that PRE pricing recommendations were not pushed to AS400. The issue was only detected during an AUD PRE review over the weekend by a Revenue Product Analyst, highlighting a serious gap in proactive QA. Although Data Engineering corrected the Alpha data within days and revenue impact was fortunately limited here, the lack of early detection exposes us to the risk of catastrophic errors going unnoticed, potentially causing significant revenue loss. Strengthening data quality monitoring and implementing rigorous QA processes is urgent to prevent future incidents.
