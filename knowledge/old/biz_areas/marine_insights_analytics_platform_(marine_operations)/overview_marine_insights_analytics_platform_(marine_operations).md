## Business Area Overview

Fleetwide AI monitoring system for complex shipboard systems (HVAC, propulsion, energy) that benchmarks performance, optimizes fuel consumption, and supports asset management and marine safety.

## Business Area Goal

Reduce net costs by an additional $2M ($4M) and lower CO2e levels by 16.5K/yr by developing a fleetwide monitoring system of complex shipboard systems (such as HVAC) on the Marine Insights Analytics Platform (MIAP) that monitors and benchmarks system performance using AI while accounting for environmental conditions. Will expand to include Asset Management & Marine Safety+Security Use-Cases.

## Business Area Impact

Reduce costs by $4M and lower CO2e by 16.5K/yr through AI-driven energy optimization.
